PRIVACY POLICY · VERSION 0.1.0

Local by design.

Quiet Switch does not transmit, sell, or share data. It has no analytics, advertising, remote code, external requests, account system, or website access. Its packaged code and assets run locally.

The management permission lets Quiet Switch read your installed extensions’ names, identifiers, icons, versions, declared permissions and enabled states, and switch eligible extensions on or off at your request. This information is displayed locally and is not sent to the developer or any other party. Chrome’s installation warning about managing apps and extensions corresponds to this core function.

The storage permission saves only extension identifiers for your Keep on exceptions and pause list, the pause timestamp, and your theme preference in chrome.storage.local. Quiet Switch does not use Chrome Sync. These preferences remain in this browser profile until changed, cleared, or the extension is uninstalled.

Quiet Switch cannot read website content, passwords, cookies, or browsing history. Permission details shown in the popup describe other extensions’ declared permissions, not website activity. The list is not retained as an analytics dataset.

Uninstall Quiet Switch to remove its local preferences. Uninstalling it does not automatically change other extensions. To clear a Keep on exception, click its shield; to clear a saved pause list while restoring the paused items, use Restore.

This policy covers Quiet Switch itself. Other extensions and Chrome operate under their own policies. This policy will be updated before any release that changes these data practices.
